# Chromopunk: Resonators — Vin's Escape

A single-file 2D action platformer set in the **Chromopunk Universe** (the *Chromopunk: Resonators* world bible, archive CR-DOM-2084).

> May 31, 2084, 23:48. The Fourfold Fall breaks the sky over Matagorda Bay.
> CBE containment fails. Three boys, one body, one night to cross Domingo City.
> *The person is the final colony.*

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build, no dependencies, no network — everything (art, audio, levels) is generated in the file. Works with keyboard or touch (mobile controls appear automatically).

## The run

You play **Vicente "Vin" Rodríguez**, CBE-03, escaping the Bloodline Meridian on the canonical opening night of the story — from the 23:54 containment failure to 02:46 beneath the Dead Rail, where Reina finds him. **Tomas** (Lightning, combat) and **Elik** (Blood, regulation) ride along as consciousness anchors and voices in your head.

Four stages, tracked against the canon clock:

| Clock | Stage | Location |
|---|---|---|
| 23:54 | CONTAINMENT | CBE Deep Levels · Bloodline Meridian |
| 00:17 | THE THRESHOLD | The Grid · Quiet Mile |
| 01:03 | CROSSING GOLD | Domingo Center · Storm Rain |
| 02:10 | THE DEAD RAIL | Linewood · Red Ring |

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| ← → / A D | Move |
| ↑ / W / Space | Jump (↓ + jump drops through platforms) |
| J / Z | Spectrum action (bolt / flame / ice shot / ground slam) |
| 1–4 or Q / E | Switch spectrum |
| K / X / Shift | Wind dash (brief invulnerability) |
| L / C / H | Blood heal (30 charge → 34 vitals) |
| P | Pause · M mute |

## Systems (straight from the world bible)

- **Seven spectra.** Lightning zaps enemies and junction boxes; Fire burns barricades; Ice freezes enemies and caps steam vents; Earth slam breaks cracked floors and staggers everything nearby; Wind is your dash; Blood converts charge into healing; Water is passive — rain vents your waste heat.
- **No miracle is free.** Every output spends Charge and builds **Rot**. At 80% you start *Flickering* (weaker regen, more damage taken); at 100% a **Redline Cascade** knocks your spectra offline. Reprieve doses and grounding circles (checkpoints) push it back down.
- **Suppression.** Grounders project a field that bleeds your charge. The Warden-class recovery walker at the Dead Rail insists that *custody is care*.
- **12 data shards** hide fragments of the archive — the Compact, the Rodríguez Letter, Tooth Nine, CITYHEART.

Best run (score/time/shards) is kept in localStorage.
